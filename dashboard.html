💡 Key Scientific Conclusions

1. Minority Collapse Diagnosis

Under Standard Cross-Entropy (C1 & C4), classifiers suffer from strict collapse on Strongly Negative examples (0 predictions, recall 0.00). Despite this complete failure on the minority class, overall raw accuracy remains deceptively high (83.33%) because models exploit majority Neutral dominance.

2. Class-Weighted Recovery

Class-Weighted Cross-Entropy ($w_0 = 7.33$) eliminates strict collapse entirely across all random seeds, restoring Strongly Negative recall from 0.00 to 0.606 (MuRIL) and 0.515 (HingRoBERTa) and driving superior Macro-F1.
